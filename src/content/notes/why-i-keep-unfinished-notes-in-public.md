I tend to learn by going down rabbit holes. Sometimes I come away with an
answer, oftentimes I come away with a better question. This garden is a place
for these discoveries to live before I forget why I was interested in them. 

As my advisor kept telling me, writing helps find the gaps in my understanding.
As Giles Thomas explains in [On the Benefits of Learning in
Public](https://www.gilesthomas.com/2025/02/20250223-til-deep-dive-posts),
explaining what you’ve learned helps it settle into your thinking. Sharing it
also gives someone else a chance to benefit from it, they may even correct a
mistake or offer another perspective.

Giles Thomas in [It's still worth blogging in the age of
AI](https://www.gilesthomas.com/2025/02/blogging-in-the-age-of-ai) also talks
about how writing remains valuable for the person doing the writing and I hope
to benefit from the same. The notes in this garden reflect my current
understanding, some will become longer posts while others will remain small.
I'll revise them as I learn more.
